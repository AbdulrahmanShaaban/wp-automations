# FREELANCE MARKET RESEARCH — for Abdelrahman Shaaban (MERN dev, Egypt)

> Researched 2026-09-29. Every number has a source and a confidence tag.
> **HIGH** = published by the platform itself or a listed company. **MED** = reputable secondary source or a live listing page. **LOW** = blog/marketing content (vendors selling services), use as a rough range only.

## 0. READ THIS FIRST — limits of this research (be honest with yourself)

1. **I could not crawl the job boards.** Upwork, Fiverr, Mostaql and Khamsat block automated access or only expose snippets. So there are **no live "N open jobs for X" counts** here. What I have: (a) the platforms' own 2026 reports, (b) public listing pages I could read (Mostaql WordPress projects page, Khamsat WordPress category), (c) vendor blogs with price ranges. For live counts use the 30-minute manual scan in §7.
2. **Almost all price data is US/Europe-centered.** Egyptian and Gulf clients pay less. Treat USD numbers as the *ceiling* you grow toward, not what your first client pays.
3. **"How much does X earn" blogs are often written by people selling courses.** I tagged them LOW.
4. Nothing here is a promise about your income. It is a map of where demand is, where it is crowded, and what to test first.

## 1. The currency reality (this changes the whole plan)

- USD/EGP was about **51.8** in mid-September 2026 (Banque Misr 51.74 buy / 51.84 sell; NBE 51.8 / 51.9 on 14 Sep 2026). **[HIGH]**
- So **50,000 EGP ≈ $965/month.**

What that means:

| How you get paid | What 50k EGP/month requires |
|---|---|
| **Egyptian clients, paid in EGP** | Simple stores are priced around 8,000–15,000 EGP, mid-range 20,000–60,000 EGP (one Egyptian agency's published price guide, ~3 months old) **[LOW–MED]**. So you need roughly **4–6 simple stores every month**, or 1–2 mid-range projects plus retainers. Hard to sustain alone. |
| **International clients, paid in USD** | About **$1,000/month** = 3–4 small projects at $250–$400, or **one automation retainer** at the low end of the published ranges ($650–$1,200/month, see §4). Much easier. |

**Conclusion:** the 50k target is *moderate in dollars, heavy in pounds*. Whatever service you pick, **at least half your clients should pay in USD/Gulf currencies** (Upwork, Fiverr, Contra, LinkedIn outreach to Gulf/European small businesses), with Egyptian clients as volume and local references.

## 2. What is actually in demand (platform data)

### 2.1 Upwork — In-Demand Skills 2026 (published 4 Feb 2026) **[HIGH]**
Method: completed jobs, freelancer earnings 2025 vs 2024, demand originating in the US.

**Top in-demand, Coding & Web Development (in order):** Full-stack development, Web design, Front-end, Mobile app, Back-end, **E-commerce website development**, UX/UI design, **Scripting & automation**, Manual testing, **CMS development**.

**Fastest-growing, Coding & Web:** **AI integration +178%**, **AI chatbot development +71%**, firmware +14%.

**Other relevant growth:** E-commerce *management* +130% (Customer service/admin category), AI video generation/editing +329%, AI image generation +95%, Logo design +44%, Marketing automation is in the top-10 Sales & Marketing skills. Skills explicitly tied to AI grew **+109%** overall vs +23% for other in-demand skills.

**Reading:** you are already in the most-demanded bucket (full-stack, e-commerce, CMS, scripting/automation). The *growth* is in **AI integration and chatbots**, i.e., wiring AI/automation into existing business systems.

### 2.2 Fiverr — Business Trends Index 2026 (published 9 Jun 2026; searches Nov 2025–Apr 2026) **[HIGH for the press release; MED for the third-party retellings below]**
- Demand for "Claude Code specialists" up **938%** in six months (searches). AI-related services growth by category: Video & Animation **+278%**, Programming & Tech **+94%**, Digital Marketing +62%, Data +3%.
- Fiverr's own investor material says AI is automating simple tasks and Fiverr is **moving upmarket toward larger, longer projects**. **[HIGH]** → the bottom rung (cheap generic tasks) is shrinking.
- Third-party retelling of Fiverr/Malt data (a freelancer-academy blog; the source itself warns it is promotional and undated) **[LOW]**: "n8n AI automation +125%", "Shopify website development +330%" but generic "Shopify experts" only +8%, "vibe coding +61%", "Base44 +95%". **Pattern:** buyers search for the **named tool + outcome**, not the generic skill. "n8n WhatsApp booking automation" beats "automation expert".
- Independent commentary: categories that used to be easy entry points (basic writing, simple logos, data entry, basic translation) are now the *hardest* places to earn on Fiverr. **[LOW]**

### 2.3 What is shrinking (avoid as your main offer)
- Generic small tasks AI can now do (basic copy, simple logo, transcription, data entry). **[MED]**
- "I make WordPress websites" as an undifferentiated offer: the Upwork WordPress median is about **$20/hour** (typical $15–28), with plenty of listed developers at $5–10/hour in Pakistan, the Philippines etc. **[HIGH: Upwork's own cost page]**. You cannot win on price.

**Reading:** the *entry* WordPress/Woo market is crowded and cheap. Money is in: **custom plugins, payment/shipping integrations, performance, and headless (WordPress + Next.js)**. Headless is your unfair advantage: you already ship Next.js/React/Node.

### 3.2 Arabic market (live listing pages) **[MED — examples, not statistics]**
- **Mostaql** has a dedicated WordPress projects page. Recent listings I could read: a WooCommerce store for a clothing brand; a Saudi accessories brand (glasses, watches, bags) needing store development/optimization; a nuts company wanting product filtering; a WooCommerce store "ready for ad campaigns and traffic". The budget filter on that page spans **$25 to $10,000**. A five-year-old closed project: WordPress store budget $500–$1,000, 30 days. → Gulf-brand e-commerce clients exist and ask for **performance + ads-readiness + product filtering**, not just "a theme".
- **Khamsat** WordPress services: Khamsat's own page says base price is **$5–$25** for a basic service, rising with add-ons. → micro-gigs; good for first reviews, bad for income.
- Mostaql freelancer profiles show sellers advertising 40+ completed WordPress/WooCommerce projects with 5/5 ratings → **you compete against established sellers with reviews.** You need a niche and 3–5 reviews before price competition stops mattering.

### 3.3 Technical facts you need for offers (verified 2026-09-29)
- **WordPress 7.1** released 19 Aug 2026; 7.0 on 20 May 2026; **7.2 planned 10 Dec 2026**. 7.0 minimum PHP 7.4, **PHP 8.2+ recommended** (8.3 commonly cited as the sweet spot). 7.1 enforces iframed editor for block themes: **blocks need Block API v3**. PHP-only block registration exists (no JS build needed). WordPress 7.0 added an AI Client, Connectors API, Abilities API. **[HIGH/MED]**
- **WooCommerce 11.1.x** current (11.1.0 on 3 Sep 2026; 11.1.2 on 22 Sep; 11.2 in pre-release). 11.1 speeds up Store API/REST requests by 30–42% on non-rendering requests, variation galleries built in. HPOS (High-Performance Order Storage) and the block Cart/Checkout are the modern defaults. **[HIGH]**
- **Paymob for WooCommerce** (Egypt/UAE/Oman/KSA/Pakistan) is an official plugin; on WooCommerce.com marked tested with WP 7.1 / WC 11.0; supports cards, wallets, Apple/Google Pay, Kiosk, BNPL (ValU, Souhoola, Halan, etc.), instalments; T+1 settlement for most methods. Listing rating on the WooCommerce Marketplace is only 2.8/5 (6 reviews) → **merchants have integration pain → a service opportunity.** **[HIGH/MED]**

| Upwork n8n expert, hourly | $40–100/hour typical (profiles shown from $10–35/hour also exist) | HIGH |
| Upwork: app-to-app workflow | $200–600 | HIGH |
| Upwork: custom integration (webhooks, transforms, error handling) | $800–2,500 | HIGH |
| Upwork: multi-system pipeline | $3,000+ | HIGH |
| Upwork: ongoing optimization/support | $800–3,000 (per engagement) | HIGH |
| Fiverr "complete workflow" listings | $45–520 | LOW |
| Retainer, monitoring + small builds (SMB) | $650–1,200/month; standard ops $1,200–2,400; live AI workflows $2,500–6,000 | LOW |
| Example project quotes | lead notification $250; WhatsApp booking bot ~$1,800 + $300/month; AI lead-qualification agent ~$2,200 + $250/month | LOW |
| Beginner path (course-seller blog) | $50–150 per simple two-app workflow to start; $200–1,500 per build later | LOW |
| Self-hosted n8n premium | one rate card (US-based, 54 invoices) says self-hosted work bills ~25–40% more. **Caution:** under n8n's free license you may install/maintain n8n on the *client's own* server for a fee, but **not host it for them on yours** (verified in n8n's License FAQ) | LOW |

**Reading:** even the *low* end of automation projects ($200–600) is at or above what a small WordPress fix pays, and **retainers ($650+/month) are the mechanism that gets you to ~$1,000/month with 1–2 clients.** Egypt-local automation will pay less, but WhatsApp/Sheets/order-notification automation is exactly what Egyptian shop owners lack.
- Who wins: people who can **self-host n8n and write code in Code nodes** (you can: Node/TS, Docker, Postgres/Redis), not drag-and-drop-only users.
- Caveat: n8n has a low entry barrier and a flood of course-takers; differentiate with **niche + outcome + reliability (error handling, logging, monitoring)**.

## 6. Recommendation (my reasoning, with the alternative)

**Options considered**
- A. **WooCommerce/WordPress builds only.** Big volume, crowded, ~$20/hr median. Good for first reviews; poor as the only plan.
- B. **Automation only.** Better rates + retainers; but you need proof and niche; competitive on Fiverr with course-takers.
- C. **Full-stack "fix and ship" (finishing apps started with AI tools/no-code).** *My inference* from Fiverr's "vibe coding +61% / Base44 +95%" signals plus Upwork's "AI integration" growth: businesses are building prototypes and need a real engineer to finish, secure and deploy them. Fits your MERN depth. Unverified niche → test it, don't bet on it.
- D. **Animation-first.** Narrow buyer base; keep as a differentiator, not the offer.

**Suggested stack of offers (positioning: "I make your online store or business site sell, and automate what happens after the order")**
1. **Core service (volume, local references):** WooCommerce/WordPress store or business site **with Egyptian/Gulf payments (Paymob, Fawry etc.), WhatsApp click-to-chat, speed & SEO** — fixed price, fixed timeline, 3 packages.
2. **Add-on (margin + recurring):** **Order & lead automation** (n8n self-hosted): new-order → WhatsApp/Telegram/Sheets, abandoned cart, review requests, stock alerts. Sold as monthly care plan.
3. **Premium (later, USD):** **Headless WordPress/Woo + Next.js** and custom plugins/integrations for international clients.
4. **Differentiator:** subtle, fast animation. Not a service line.

**First 90 days test plan**
| Weeks | Do | Success signal |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Learn WordPress/Woo basics + build 1 own demo store with Paymob **test mode**; set up n8n on a cheap VPS | Demo store + 2 n8n workflows working |
| 2–4 | 2–3 low-priced jobs for real local businesses (New Valley, Assiut, Cairo) in exchange for written reviews and permission to show the work | 3 reviews/testimonials |
| 3–8 | 5–10 personalized outreach messages/day to shops that sell via Instagram/Facebook/WhatsApp; 3–5 Upwork proposals/day for Woo fixes + n8n jobs | ≥2 paid projects/month |
| 6–12 | Convert every project into a care plan (maintenance + automation); raise prices after review #5 | ≥2 recurring clients |

**Kill criteria:** if after ~60 days of *consistent* outreach (not tinkering) you have 0 paid conversations from 150+ targeted messages, the problem is offer/positioning, not skills — change the niche or the message, don't add technologies.

**One tension to face:** in your earlier notes you wanted to position as a consulting-style developer, not compete on low-cost builds. WooCommerce small stores *are* the low-price segment. The way out is what §6 does: use them for **references and volume**, and price **automation + integrations + performance** as the consulting-grade layer.

## 7. Manual market scan you (or opencode with the chrome-devtools MCP) can run in 30–60 minutes

Do this per platform, record in a sheet (`date, platform, query, job title, budget, currency, client country, posted, proposals count, skills, link`). Take **20 listings per query**, newest first.

**Queries (Upwork / Freelancer / Fiverr search)**
`woocommerce`, `woocommerce payment gateway`, `woocommerce speed`, `wordpress headless nextjs`, `n8n`, `n8n whatsapp`, `automation workflow`, `zapier make migration`, `ai chatbot`, `shopify`, `fix ai generated app`, `next.js`.
**Queries (Mostaql / Khamsat, Arabic)**
`ووكومرس`, `متجر إلكتروني`, `ووردبريس`, `بوابة دفع`, `أتمتة`, `شات بوت واتساب`, `n8n`, `تحسين سرعة موقع`.

**Compute after collecting:** median budget; % of jobs with budget < $100; how many proposals on average (competition); top 10 repeated skills; recurring pain points; client countries. **Decide by:** high budget × low proposals × repeated pain you can fix.
**Rules:** don't apply during the scan; don't scrape behind logins; respect each site's terms; if a site blocks automation, do it by hand.
