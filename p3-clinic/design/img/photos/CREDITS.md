# CREDITS — photography in this project

All eight frames are **Unsplash** photographs downloaded into this repo (so the site never
hotlinks and never breaks if Unsplash changes). Unsplash's licence allows free commercial
use with **no attribution required** — which is what "no copyright" means in practice here:
not public domain, but no fee, no licence key, no per-seat cost, no attribution obligation.

**Deliberately excluded:** anything from `plus.unsplash.com` / `premium_photo-*` (Unsplash+,
paid) and anything from iStock (paid stock). The Unsplash search page surfaces both next to
the free results, so the filter is `images.unsplash.com/photo-…` only.

## Honesty rules that still apply to these photos

The clinic is fictional, so:

- Every photo caption on the page says it is **توضيحية** (illustrative) and the page keeps its
  **Demo** badge.
- No photo is presented as "our patient before/after" and no photo carries a medical claim.
- The team photos are procedure/tool shots and one consultation scene — they illustrate a
  specialty, they are not portraits claiming to be real employees of a real clinic.
- For the real (non-demo) build the owner must supply photos of their own clinic. Stock
  photography of someone else's practice would be the one thing in this project that a client
  could not legally ship.

## The one drawn asset that stayed

`favicon.svg` / `logo-mark.svg` are original (generated by `design/logo.mjs`): a white tooth with
three light rays on the palette's blue. A stock or third-party clinic logo would be someone
else's trademark. The same tooth outline also appears inline in `preview-a.html` as the
watermark inside the prescription card, because it has to animate.
