## Owner additions after approval (2026-10-06)

1. **Animation is required** — not a static page. One signature moment (the drawn rule) plus
   restrained supporting motion, all `prefers-reduced-motion` aware, no CLS.
2. **Real imagery, no text boxes** — placeholders that only contain words ("صورة (Demo)")
   are not acceptable. Shipped as Unsplash photography (free commercial licence) plus an
   original logo. Unsplash and Pexels both refuse programmatic access from this machine
   (401 / 403), so the frames were harvested from the rendered search page in the browser and
   downloaded; the Wikimedia Commons fallback was rejected because its results for
   "dental clinic" are 1914–1946 archive scans.

## Motion spec (implemented in `preview-a.html`, reused by the real build)

- Hero on load: headline lines mask up (60ms stagger) → the rule draws (scaleX) → the
  prescription card rises 12px → the stamp presses in with a −2° rotation.
- On scroll (ScrollTrigger, `once: true`): section heads, service rows (40ms stagger),
  team cards, price rows and the illustration band fade + rise 16px.
- Micro: image frames scale 1.02 on hover; the nav gains a shadow after 40px of scroll.
- Budget: ≤1 signature + 4 supporting rules. Nothing loops. Nothing moves on hover except
  the two listed. Durations 240–700ms, `power2.out` / `power3.out` only.

**Motion** — GSAP 3.12.5 + ScrollTrigger from CDN, gated behind a `.js` class that is only
added once GSAP is confirmed loaded, so a CDN failure leaves the page fully readable.

**Three bugs found while verifying (all fixed, all would have shipped silently):**

1. **Duplicate SVG attributes = invisible image.** The art helpers emitted their own
   `stroke-width`/`opacity` and the caller's `extra` added a second one; duplicate attributes
   make the XML invalid and the browser renders nothing (no alt text either — it just shows
   blank). Two rounds hit this (stroke-width, then opacity). The generator that caused it is
   gone; what remains (`logo.mjs`) is XML-validated after every run.
2. **`ScrollTrigger` `start: 'top 88%'` never fires for the last block.** The page cannot
   scroll far enough to bring the contact cards 12% below the fold, so they stayed at
   `opacity: 0` forever. Changed to `start: 'top bottom-=60'`.
3. **Triggers measured before the page settled.** The webfont swap and image decoding change
   the page height after creation, so `ScrollTrigger.refresh()` now runs on `document.fonts.ready`
   and on `window.load`.

Verification on the shipped version: 12/12 reveal targets reach opacity 1, `scrollWidth -
clientWidth` = 0 at 390 and 1440, all 9 images load with explicit `width`/`height` (no CLS),
console clean, and `prefers-reduced-motion` returns before the `.js` class is ever added.
